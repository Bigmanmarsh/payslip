The checker can see

  • Every payment received by this salary account
  • Amounts, dates and payslip contents
  • Which payslips are genuinely signed by an employer

The checker cannot see

  • Your personal wallet or other accounts
  • Where you send money from this account
  • Your spending or current balance
  • Anything about other workers

Tip: only receive salary in this account. Anything paid into it will be visible to whoever holds your proof code.
